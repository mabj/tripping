# Rome

## General Tips

- You will need to book in advance and buy the ticket online if you want to visit the Vatican Museum (and avoid long lines);
- Watch out for pick-pocketing! Specially in the city center (Vatican, Coliseum, Trevi Fountain, Piazza Navona etc);
- Avoid to drive in Rome - you can do all main touristic attractions by foot, metro or by bus;
- Rome has 3 metro lines (A, B and C). Stadard single travel ticket costs €1,50, it lasts for 100 minutes and it is also valid for busses;
- Avoid places to eat around main touristic attactions (coliseum, vaticano etc);
- If travelling during summer (June, July and Aug) bring sun blocker and sun glasses, it can get really hot;
- Book your accommodation on places outside the city center (e.g. Monteverde, Ostiense, Testaccio, Gianicolense);

## Things to Do

- Eat a Roman-style Pizza and a Carbonara (see "Places to Eat" section);
- Walk around the margins of River Tiber;
- Visit the main fountains in Rome (Fontana di Trevi, Fontana dei Quattro Fiumi, Fontanone del Gianicolo, Fontana del Nettuno, Fontana del Tritone, Fontana del Moro, Fontana della Barcaccia);
- Nightlife and cocktail bar in Pigneto;
- Visit the Vatican Museum, Basilica di San Pietro and check it out the Creation of Adam inside the Sistine Chapel (and all Michelangelo's);
- Check a nice sunset view of the city from the Gianicolo Belvedere;
- Climbing the stairs up to Piazza del Campidoglio

## Loops

### Loop 1 - Colisseum -> Vatican

<pre>
Colisseum -> Foro Romano -> Monumento a Vittorio Emanuele II -> Fontana di Treve -> Pantheon -> Piazza Navona -> Castel Sant'Angelo -> Vatican -> Piazza San Pietro
</pre>

### Loop 2 - Piazza di Spagna -> Fontana dell'Acqua Paola -> Villa Doria Pamphili

This loop has some elevation. Maybe better to take a bus from Castel Sant'Angelo to Fontana dell'Acqua Paola and another one to Villa Doria Pamphili. Spend time exploring Villa Doria Pamphili, it is a beautiful green area with Roman ruins and beautiful and iconic Roman Pine trees.

<pre>
Piazza di Spagna -> Palazzo Borghese -> Castel Sant'Angelo -> Fontana dell'Acqua Paola -> Villa Doria Pamphili
</pre>
