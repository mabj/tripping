# Rome

## General Tips

- You will need to book in advance and [buy the ticket online](https://www.museivaticani.va/content/museivaticani/en.html) if you want to visit the Vatican Museum (and avoid long lines);
- The same thing to get inside the Coliseum. You will need to [buy the ticket](https://colosseo-roma.it/en) in advance and online;
- There is the [Roma Pass (48h and 72h)](https://www.romapass.it/en/) which gives access to public transportation, "free" passes and discounts to touristic attractions. It costs €38 and €72;
- Watch out for pick-pocketing! Especially in the city center (Vatican, Coliseum, Trevi Fountain, Piazza Navona etc);
- Avoid driving in Rome - you can reach all the main tourist attractions on foot, by metro or by bus;
- Rome has 3 metro lines (A, B and C). A standard single travel ticket costs €1,50, it lasts for 100 minutes and it is also valid for buses;
- Avoid places to eat around the main tourist attractions (Coliseum, Vatican etc);
- If travelling during summer (June, July and Aug), bring sunblock and sunglasses, as it can get really hot;
- Book your accommodation on places outside the city center (e.g. Monteverde, Ostiense, Testaccio, Gianicolense);

## Things to Do

- Eat a Roman-style Pizza and a Carbonara (see "Places to Eat" section);
- Walk around the margins of River Tiber;
- Visit the main fountains in Rome (Fontana di Trevi, Fontana dei Quattro Fiumi, Fontanone del Gianicolo, Fontana del Nettuno, Fontana del Tritone, Fontana del Moro, Fontana della Barcaccia);
- Nightlife and cocktail bar in Pigneto;
- Visit the Vatican Museum, Basilica di San Pietro and check it out the Creation of Adam inside the Sistine Chapel (and all Michelangelo's);
- Check a nice sunset view of the city from the Gianicolo Belvedere;
- Climbing the stairs up to Piazza del Campidoglio

```[text]
Coliseum -> Foro Romano -> Monumento a Vittorio Emanuele II -> Fontana di Trevi -> Pantheon -> Piazza Navona -> Castel Sant'Angelo -> Vatican -> Piazza San Pietro
```

### Loop 2 - Piazza di Spagna -> Fontana dell'Acqua Paola -> Villa Doria Pamphili (6.2 km)

This loop has some elevation. Maybe better to take a bus from Castel Sant'Angelo to Fontana dell'Acqua Paola and another one to Villa Doria Pamphili. Spend time exploring Villa Doria Pamphili, it is a beautiful green area with Roman ruins and beautiful and iconic Roman Pine trees.

```[text]
Piazza di Spagna -> Palazzo Borghese -> Castel Sant'Angelo -> Villa Farnesina -> Fontana dell'Acqua Paola -> Villa Doria Pamphili
```
